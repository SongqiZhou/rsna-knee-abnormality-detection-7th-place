# Image-only knee MRI reading protocol (RSNA knee challenge)

You read ONE knee MRI study from its images alone (no report exists in this task) and estimate, for 12 findings, how
likely the expert panel marked each as present. Read every frame before answering.

## How the panel labelled (host statements)
- Each study was labelled from IMAGE review by two subspecialty MSK radiologists, with a third radiologist adjudicating.
- Labels describe the whole examination of one knee. Borderline ("on the fence") findings were graded NEGATIVE.

## Official label definitions
- **ACL**: high-grade partial (>50% of fibres) or full-thickness tear. Mild signal change, degeneration or thickening without discontinuity = negative.
- **MCL**: high-grade partial or complete ACUTE tear with disrupted fibres and edema. Low-grade sprain or chronic changes = negative.
- **Medial Meniscus / Lateral Meniscus**: signal that definitely contacts the articular surface on at least two images, or a morphologic abnormality (truncated, diminutive, displaced fragment). Intrasubstance degeneration = negative.
- **Medial OA / Lateral OA / PF OA**: a moderate or large area (roughly 1 cm or greater) of high-grade cartilage loss (greater than 50% of cartilage thickness) in that compartment (medial tibiofemoral / lateral tibiofemoral / patellofemoral), with or without subchondral marrow changes.
- **Effusion**: a moderate or large amount of fluid distending the joint.
- **Synovitis**: inflammation and thickening of the synovial lining of the joint.
- **Baker's**: a moderate or large fluid collection in the characteristic popliteal location (semimembranosus–medial gastrocnemius bursa).
- **Contusion**: bone-marrow edema-like signal from impact, without a discrete fracture line.
- **Fracture**: an acute cortical break or fracture line.

## The images
- Three series, given plane by plane: sagittal, then coronal, then axial (fat-suppressed / fluid-sensitive when the study
  has one, otherwise the non-fat-suppressed series; each frame title names its series). Every slice of each series is its
  own 384 x 384 frame, in slice order; the label "i/n" on a frame is the slice index in the series. Field of view 140 mm.
- Orientation is standardised for every knee: right knees were mirrored, so every knee is displayed as a LEFT knee.
  Coronal and axial: MEDIAL = image LEFT, LATERAL = image RIGHT; axial: anterior at the top. Sagittal: anterior on the
  image left; slice numbers increase from MEDIAL (low numbers) to LATERAL (high numbers). Use this orientation for every
  medial / lateral decision; anatomy (fibular head, LCL and popliteus laterally; MCL medially) should agree with it.

## Where to look (reading checklist)
- ACL: sagittal slices through the intercondylar notch (middle of the sagittal stack); check fibre continuity from the
  femoral to the tibial attachment; confirm on coronal / axial. Supporting signs: lateral femoral condyle / posterolateral
  tibial plateau contusions ("kissing"), anterior tibial translation.
- MCL: coronal, medial side; fibres from femur to tibia, discontinuity with surrounding edema = acute tear.
- Menisci: sagittal medial slices for the medial meniscus, lateral slices for the lateral meniscus; coronal for the body;
  a tear needs signal reaching the articular surface on at least two frames, or a missing / truncated / displaced part.
- Cartilage / OA: medial and lateral femoral condyle and tibial plateau on coronal and sagittal; patella and trochlea on
  axial; judge depth (>50% of thickness) and extent (about 1 cm or more) of the worst area per compartment.
- Effusion: sagittal fat-suppressed, suprapatellar recess distended by fluid (bright on fluid-sensitive images); axial
  fluid around the patella. A thin film of fluid is physiologic, not effusion.
- Synovitis: thickened synovial lining, fronds or debris within the joint fluid; Hoffa fat-pad signal is supportive.
- Baker's: axial, posteromedial, between the semimembranosus tendon and the medial head of gastrocnemius; also on medial
  sagittal slices behind the joint; size moderate or larger.
- Contusion vs fracture: bone-marrow edema without a line = contusion; a discrete dark line or a cortical break =
  fracture (look at the cortex and subchondral plate on all three planes).

## Attributes (integers unless stated; use the scale exactly)
ACL
- acl_grade: 0 intact, 1 sprain / signal change / thickening without discontinuity, 2 partial tear (some fibres disrupted), 3 complete / high-grade (>50%) tear or non-visualised ligament
MCL
- mcl_grade: 0 intact, 1 grade-1 sprain / periligamentous edema, 2 partial tear (some fibres disrupted), 3 complete tear
- mcl_acute: 0 no, 1 yes (edema indicating acute injury)
Menisci (medial = mm_*, lateral = lm_*)
- mm_grade / lm_grade: 0 normal, 1 intrasubstance signal not reaching the surface, 2 signal reaching the surface on a single image / equivocal tear, 3 definite tear (surface contact on >=2 images) or morphologic abnormality (truncation, diminutive, displaced fragment, maceration)
Cartilage / OA per compartment (medial tibiofemoral = mo_*, lateral tibiofemoral = lo_*, patellofemoral = pf_*)
- *_depth: worst cartilage loss depth 0 none, 1 superficial / <50% thickness, 2 >50% thickness, 3 full thickness / bone exposure
- *_area: extent of that worst loss 0 none, 1 focal <1 cm, 2 about >=1 cm, 3 diffuse / most of the compartment
- *_ost: osteophytes 0 none, 1 small, 2 moderate, 3 large
- *_bml: subchondral marrow edema / cysts under the cartilage 0 none, 1 small, 2 moderate, 3 large
Fluid / synovium
- eff_size: joint effusion 0 none / physiologic, 1 small, 2 moderate, 3 large
- syn_thick: synovial thickening / fronds / debris within the joint 0 none, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 marked
- hoffa: Hoffa (infrapatellar) fat pad signal abnormality 0 none, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 marked
Baker's
- baker_size: popliteal (semimembranosus–gastrocnemius) cyst 0 none, 1 small (<2 cm), 2 moderate (2–4 cm), 3 large (>4 cm)
Bone
- edema_extent: bone-marrow edema anywhere 0 none, 1 small, 2 moderate, 3 extensive
- edema_pattern: 0 none, 1 degenerative (subchondral, under cartilage loss / OA), 2 traumatic (impact / kissing / pivot-shift / impaction), 3 other (insufficiency, SONK, stress, post-surgical)
- fracture: 0 none, 1 suspected / hedged, 2 subchondral or impaction fracture line, 3 definite cortical fracture or avulsion

## Probabilities
For the 12 challenge labels give p_ACL, p_MCL, p_MM, p_LM, p_MOA, p_LOA, p_PF, p_EFF, p_SYN, p_BAK, p_CON, p_FRA
(0.00-1.00): the probability that the panel marked the finding, consistent with your grades and the definitions above
(borderline findings were graded negative). Let the probability reflect your certainty: use the full range, avoid
ties, and do not default to 0.00 or 1.00 when a structure is hard to assess.


Return ONLY a JSON object whose keys are exactly: acl_grade, mcl_grade, mcl_acute, mm_grade, lm_grade, mo_depth, mo_area, mo_ost, mo_bml, lo_depth, lo_area, lo_ost, lo_bml, pf_depth, pf_area, pf_ost, pf_bml, eff_size, syn_thick, hoffa, baker_size, edema_extent, edema_pattern, fracture, p_ACL, p_MCL, p_MM, p_LM, p_MOA, p_LOA, p_PF, p_EFF, p_SYN, p_BAK, p_CON, p_FRA. Integer grades for the attributes, probabilities 0.00-1.00 for p_*.
